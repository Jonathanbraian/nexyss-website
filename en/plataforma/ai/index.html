The problem

An AI that needs a tab
arrives after the work is done.

An AI bolted onto each tool asks you to leave the record, explain the context and come back to copy the answer. The result is an AI used when there is spare time, not when the decision is made.

One more tab to open

Leaving the record to ask a question interrupts the work. The question arrives without the context of whoever is doing it.

Each tool with its own AI

One system's assistant does not know the other's records. Each answers with the piece it can see.

The answer stays outside the history

What the AI suggested or did sits in a separate conversation, far from the record where the work happened.

Capabilities

Four capability groups,
inside the same record.

Nexyss AI understands what arrives, accelerates what is written, monitors what changes and helps decide what to do. In every module, on the same record.

The features below are on the roadmap. Founding Customers take part in defining the delivery order.

Understands

Understand what arrives and take it to the right record

Messages, documents and requests become structured data where the work happens.

  • Roadmap Understanding of requests in natural language, in the context of the module
  • Roadmap Classification of messages and records by type and priority
  • Roadmap Extraction of data from documents into the record's fields
  • Roadmap Search by meaning across the records the user can see
Accelerates

Write and summarise without leaving the record

Drafts and summaries are born from the record itself, ready for review.

  • Roadmap Drafts of messages, proposals and adverts from the record
  • Roadmap Summaries of accounts, conversations, campaigns and candidates
  • Roadmap Suggested completion of a record's fields
  • Roadmap Answers to questions about the user's data, in natural language
Monitors

Notice what changed before the report

Signals, scores and alerts calculated on what the organisation already records.

  • Roadmap Anomaly detection in entries, pipeline and attendance
  • Roadmap Score per record: account, student, lead and candidate
  • Roadmap Risk and outcome forecasting per record
  • Roadmap Alerts sent to whoever is responsible for the record, with the origin of the signal
Decides

From signal to next step

Recommendations and agents that propose and carry out the next step, with the history in the same place.

  • Roadmap Next-step recommendation per record
  • Roadmap Optimisation of priorities and allocation across queues
  • Roadmap An agent that carries out the next step across modules
  • Roadmap Every action carried out recorded in the same place as the rest of the history
In the record

The answer and the action stay where the work happens.

Nexyss AI operates inside the same record you see. What it reads, what it suggests and what it carries out stays in the same place as everything else, instead of in a separate conversation.
