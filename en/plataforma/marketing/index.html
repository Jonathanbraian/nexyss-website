The problem

Your marketing is not wrong.
It just cannot see the end of the story.

Most marketing teams run on tools that talk to neither sales nor finance. The result is not a lack of campaigns - it is a lack of a calculation that closes.

The lead changes hands and loses its memory

Every form becomes a file, every file becomes a spreadsheet. By the time the contact reaches the salesperson, the context of everything they have read and clicked has been left behind.

Marketing measures clicks, the company measures revenue

The campaign report ends where the contract begins. Nobody closes the books between what was invested and what came back - and next quarter's budget becomes opinion.

Each channel, a different tool

Email in one place, ads in another, pages in a third. Three dashboards, three definitions of “qualified lead” and no single view of the contact.

Resources
