The shared layer

What the modules have in common
is the product.

Exchanging data between systems is integration. Being born from the same data is something else. Four layers that every module inherits the day it is activated - no configuration, no project, no vendor in the middle.

01 · Data
A customer is a single record
There is no synchronisation because there is no second copy. What Finance sees is literally the same record that CRM edits.
02 · Identity
One login, one session
Nobody manages access in six places. Joining, leaving and changing role happen once and apply across the whole platform.
03 · Permission
The rule is defined once
Who sees what, by unit, region and country. It applies in every module, including those you activate later.
04 · Intelligence
AI reads the entire operation
Nexyss AI does not receive a slice through an integration. It reads everything the organisation records, in every module, all the time.

How it works

A student enrols.
See what happens without anyone lifting a finger.

In an assembled stack, this event becomes four manual tasks and three reconciliation spreadsheets. Here it is a single event, propagated.

Event
Enrolment confirmed
Atlas Academy · Dublin
Nexyss Education
Place filled, class updated and student timetable generated
Nexyss Finance
Tuition fee created in EUR, with a payment plan and collection scheduled
Nexyss CRM
Institution account updated and deal moved to won
Nexyss AI
Revenue forecast and dropout risk recalculated instantly

It applies to any event: a closed contract (CRM → Finance → HR), a new employee (HR → all), a qualified lead (Marketing → CRM). The pattern is the same, the platform is the same.

Integrations & API

What is a Nexyss module needs
no connector at all.

Integration stops being the glue between your areas and becomes just the bridge to whatever sits outside. It is far less surface to maintain.

The features below are on the roadmap. Founding Customers take part in defining the delivery order.

01 · Inside
Between modules: zero

Native connectors
