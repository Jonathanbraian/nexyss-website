The problem

Your CRM is not wrong.
It is just on its own.

Most commercial teams run on a CRM that does not talk to finance, cannot see marketing and does not know what support promised. The result is not a lack of data - it is an excess of versions of it.

The truth lives in three places

The contract value is in the CRM, the invoice is in the ERP and the promise is in an email. Nobody knows which of the three is right - and every meeting starts by reconciling a spreadsheet.

The forecast is born out of date

The spreadsheet put together on Monday is no longer valid on Wednesday. But the board decides with Monday's number, because it is the only one that exists.

Every unit starts again from zero

New branch, new process, new custom fields, new currency. Consolidating stops being a report and becomes a project with a deadline and a budget.

Resources

Everything a commercial team does -
in a single system.

It is not a CRM with integrations. It is a CRM born inside the same database as your organisation's finance, marketing, HR and education.

Pipeline

One funnel, all units

A pipeline that runs across teams, units and countries, with its own currency and target at each level - and automatic consolidation above.

  • Active Stages configurable per team, without duplicating the process
  • Active Targets by salesperson, unit, region and global
  • Active Currency conversion at the rate on the closing date
360º Accounts

Everything the company knows about the customer, on one screen

Deals, invoices, campaigns, tickets and enrolments in the same record. Coming from the modules - not from an overnight integration.

  • Active Unified history of every interaction, by source
  • Roadmap Health score calculated with data from every module
  • Active Group, head office and branch hierarchy
Automation

Work that does not need people

Rules that move a stage, create a task and fire an alert when the SLA tightens.

  • Active Visual builder, no code
  • Active Triggers across modules
  • Roadmap Audit trail of every execution
Forecast

A forecast that updates itself

Recalculated with every movement in the funnel, in three scenarios.

  • Active Committed, likely and optimistic
  • Active Comparison with the same point in the previous cycle
  • Roadmap Closing recognised directly in Finance
Global

Global by default

Multi-currency, multi-time-zone and multilingual from the first record - not a separate module.

  • Active Portuguese, English and Spanish in the interface
  • Roadmap Data residency by region
  • Active Permissions by unit and by country
