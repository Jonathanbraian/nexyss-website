The problem

Your organisation is not out of date.
It is scattered.

In an operation with several systems, each area keeps its own version of the person. The headcount that reaches the board is yesterday's, and the cost of people is a bridging spreadsheet.

Each system has its own version of the person

Recruitment, payroll, access and goals keep separate records. When someone changes area, someone has to remember to tell the others.

Headcount is a report, not a state

The number the board quotes was extracted from some system on some day. Between the extraction and the decision, the organisation has already changed.

The person starts over at every stage

The candidate retypes their details at hiring and the history is lost on exit. Rehiring means starting from scratch.

Resources

The whole people cycle,
in the same record.

It is not an HR system beside the others. It is the module in which a person is born as a candidate and on which every other module comes to depend: access, goals, cost and payroll.
