The problem

Your network has grown.
Your systems have not grown with it.

Each campus solved its own problem with its own tool. Today the network operator has eight closing spreadsheets, three definitions of “active student” and no quick answer when the board asks how the network is doing.

The student's history lives in pieces

The registrar's office has the academic record, finance has the tuition fee, coordination has attendance. Reconstructing a student's journey has become archaeology.

Every campus is an island

Same institution, different processes, different calendars, reports that do not add up. Consolidating the network becomes an end-of-semester project.

Dropout shows up too late

By the time the student stops paying or disappears from class, the signal had existed for months - scattered across systems that do not talk to each other.

Resources

An institution does a great many things.
All of it is the same platform.

From the applicant's first contact to issuing the graduate's transcript - without changing systems, exporting spreadsheets or reconciling numbers at month-end.
