Origin

Before it was a platform, it was an operation.

We spent more than ten years inside a real education operation. In that time, every new tool promised to solve one problem and created two: one more tab, one more login, one more spreadsheet to stitch together what the systems did not connect.

We learned what really weighs: data that is born in one place and dies in another, the team that becomes the integrator, the report nobody can close.

The market sells shiny apps, one for each pain, and the result is a pile of systems that your team has to keep connected to one another. Nexyss does the opposite: modules born in the same record, so that nobody has to stitch.

We rolled up our sleeves before writing the first line of code. That is why Nexyss solves what the operation asks for, not what the software finds pretty.

Questions

Common questions.

The answers we hear most before a first conversation.

Is Nexyss just software?
No. The platform is software. Nexyss Consulting is the service that decides what changes, implements and operates alongside your team. Each can be explored separately.
Can I start with one module?
Yes. The platform does not require full adoption: you start with the module for your most urgent pain and add the others when it makes sense.
Which modules exist?
Nexyss CRM, Education, Marketing, Finance, HR, AI and Integrations, plus Nexyss Consulting as a service. Each module's page shows what is active, in beta or on the roadmap.
Does everything on the site already exist?
Not necessarily. Each feature has a status, and most of the modules are under construction. We say so on every page, without hiding it.
How does pricing work?
Sold by project and made to measure, with no list price on the site. The first conversation is for understanding the scope.
What AI does Nexyss use?
Nexyss AI is a layer that runs across the modules and is under construction. We use market-available AI models, chosen per task, and we do not publish the vendor or the model.
How does a conversation start?
With a consultative conversation of 30 to 60 minutes. If there is a fit, the proposal follows within 7 working days.
How quickly do you respond?
Within 24 working hours, during Dublin business hours (GMT). We serve customers in Latin America and Europe.
