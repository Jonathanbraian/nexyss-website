The problem

The software arrives. The decision about what changes has not.

After choosing the platform, the hard part is usually human: design, switchover and routine. That is where consulting comes in.

Nobody has the full design

More than one system, more than one area and nobody who owns the whole. Each team knows its own part.

The new system lives alongside the old one

Without a cut-over plan, the switch becomes two systems running at the same time, indefinitely.

Deployed is not the same as operated

After deployment, someone has to maintain, adjust and teach. Agreeing this beforehand avoids surprises later.

The four practices

Four practices, one natural order.

Each practice can be engaged on its own. Together, they cover everything from diagnosis to operation. The scope of each is agreed with you.

01 · Strategy

Strategy & Transformation

Before implementing, decide what changes. Organisational diagnosis, order of entry and redesign of the processes the platform will support.

  1. Map how the operation works today: processes, systems and who decides.
  2. Define what the platform takes on, in what order, and what stays out.
  3. Deliver a plan your team can carry out, with someone accountable for each stage.

When it makes senseWhen more than one system or area is involved and nobody has the full design.

02 · Implementation

Implementation & Enablement

Platform deployment, legacy system migration, integration with the existing stack and support through the switchover.

  1. Prepare the environment and bring across the data that will be used.
  2. Connect what remains outside, with the current system still running.
  3. Train and support the switchover at your team's pace.

When it makes senseWhen there is a legacy system to replace or to keep for a while.

03 · Growth

Growth & Performance

Marketing, sales and operations consulting, with small squads alongside your team and sector playbooks.

  1. Choose a front: marketing, sales or operations.
  2. Set up a small squad with people from your team.
  3. Run short cycles and record the learning in a playbook.

When it makes senseWhen the platform is already running and the question has become "what to improve first".

04 · Operations

Managed Services

Assisted platform operation, support and ongoing follow-up, with the division of work agreed.

  1. Agree what Nexyss operates and what your team operates.
  2. Follow the platform day to day.
  3. Review with the customer periodically.

When it makes senseWhen the team does not want to, or cannot, maintain the operation alone.

How it works

A project has stages.
Each one has someone accountable.

At each stage, what Nexyss does and what your team does are written down before starting. When the agreement changes, it changes in writing, on both sides.

When it makes sense

Not every project needs consulting.

The platform was designed to reduce implementation work. In some cases it is still there, and that is what consulting is for.

01 · When it makes sense
When the conversation is worthwhile
There is a system switch. Several areas depend on the same data. Your team wants the platform working without becoming experts in it.
02 · We never
What we do not do
We never promise a result. We never start without an agreed scope. We never take over the operation without agreeing who owns what.

Who it is for

Who decides, who executes.

The conversation starts with whoever decides what changes and continues with whoever will carry out the switchover.

For the board and the CEO

Decide what changes and follow along without the implementation becoming a project with nobody accountable.

For operations

A switchover plan with stages, people responsible and what stays out, written down before starting.

For the CFO and the CHRO

What will be migrated, in what order and with what follow-up, with no surprises along the way.
