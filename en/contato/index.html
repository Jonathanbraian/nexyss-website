Frequently asked questions

Before you write, this may help.

How quickly do you respond?
We respond within 24 working hours, at the email address you provide. We are available during Dublin business hours (GMT).
Do you serve companies outside Europe?
Yes. We have customers in Latin America and Europe. We operate remotely with flexible time zones for meetings.
What is the onboarding process?
It starts with a consultative conversation (30-60 min) to understand operations, pain points and priorities. If there is a fit, we prepare a customised proposal within 7 working days.
Do I need to know everything I want before talking to you?
No. Many of the best partners arrive with “we know we need to improve, but we do not know where to start”. The first conversation is for that - we help map it out.
